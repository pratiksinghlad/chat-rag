## SECTION 1 — WELCOME & COMPANY OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Welcome Message

Welcome to the organization. This handbook outlines the policies, values, and expectations that guide our work culture. Every employee is expected to read and acknowledge this document within the first 5 business days of joining.

### 1.2 Company History

Founded in 2011, the company began as a three-person startup focused on enterprise data infrastructure. Over 15 years, it has grown to over 3,400 employees across 12 countries, serving Fortune 500 clients in financial services, healthcare, and logistics.

### 1.3 Mission Statement

To build software that is reliable, secure, and deeply human-centric — enabling our clients to focus on what they do best while we handle the complexity of technology at scale.

### 1.4 Core Values

- **Integrity First:** We act with honesty even when no one is watching.
- **Customer Obsession:** Every decision is filtered through the lens of client impact.
- **Radical Transparency:** We share context freely and communicate without ambiguity.
- **Ownership Mindset:** Every employee is empowered and accountable for outcomes.
- **Continuous Learning:** We invest in skills, curiosity, and professional growth daily.

### 1.5 Organizational Structure

The company operates in three business units: Product Engineering, Client Solutions, and Corporate Services. Each unit is led by a Senior Vice President reporting directly to the CEO.

## SECTION 2 — EMPLOYMENT POLICIES

### 2.1 Equal Opportunity Employment

The company is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, national origin, or any other protected class. All hiring, promotion, and compensation decisions are merit-based.

### 2.2 Employment Categories

Employees are classified as: Full-Time Permanent, Part-Time, Fixed-Term Contract, or Contractor/Consultant. Benefits eligibility varies by category and is detailed in the Benefits Guide on the HR portal.

### 2.3 Background Verification

All offers are contingent on successful completion of background verification including identity check, employment history, educational credential verification, and criminal record check where legally permitted.

### 2.4 Probation Period

New employees serve a 90-day probation period. During this period, either party may terminate employment with 7 days' notice. Performance checkpoints occur at Day 30 and Day 60.

### 2.5 Internal Transfers

Employees may apply for internal openings after completing 12 months in their current role. Transfer requests must be initiated through the HR portal and require the current manager's approval before the application proceeds.

### 2.6 Termination Policy

Voluntary resignation requires a minimum notice period of 30 days for individual contributors and 60 days for managers and above. Exit interviews are conducted by HR and are strictly confidential.

## SECTION 3 — COMPENSATION & BENEFITS

### 3.1 Salary Structure

Compensation is benchmarked annually against industry salary surveys. Roles are slotted into pay bands (B1 through B9) and reviewed during the annual compensation cycle each January.

### 3.2 Payroll Schedule

Employees are paid on the last working day of each month via direct bank transfer. Payslips are available digitally through the HR self-service portal by the 25th of each month.

### 3.3 Health Insurance

The company provides comprehensive health insurance covering the employee, spouse, and up to two dependent children. Coverage includes hospitalization, outpatient consultations, dental, and vision with a family floater of $50,000 per year.

### 3.4 Retirement & Pension

Employees are enrolled in the company 401(k) plan from Day 1. The company matches contributions up to 4% of base salary. Vesting is on a 3-year graded schedule: 33% Year 1, 66% Year 2, 100% Year 3.

### 3.5 Learning & Development Budget

Each employee receives an annual L&D budget of $1,500 for courses, certifications, conferences, and books. Unused budgets do not carry over to the next calendar year. Claims are submitted via the HR portal.

### 3.6 Employee Stock Options (ESOPs)

All permanent employees at Band B4 and above are eligible for ESOP grants. Vesting is over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Details are provided in the individual offer letter and ESOP agreement.

### 3.7 Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Leave can be taken any time within the first 12 months after birth or adoption.

## SECTION 4 — WORKPLACE CONDUCT

### 4.1 Code of Conduct

All employees must behave professionally, ethically, and lawfully at all times — whether in the office, on client premises, at company events, or representing the company online.

### 1.0 Anti-Harassment Policy

Harassment of any form — verbal, physical, visual, or digital — is strictly prohibited. This includes unwanted sexual advances, discriminatory jokes, and intimidation. Violations will lead to immediate disciplinary action up to and including termination.

### 4.3 Conflict of Interest

Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflicts of interest to HR and their manager immediately. This includes outside employment, investments in competitors, or personal relationships with vendors.

### 4.4 Confidentiality Obligations

Employees must protect all non-public company information — including client data, product roadmaps, and financial results — during and indefinitely after employment. Violations may result in legal action.

### 4.5 Social Media Guidelines

Employees may not post confidential company information on any public platform. Opinions shared on personal accounts must include a disclaimer that views are personal and not those of the employer.

### 4.6 Drug and Alcohol Policy

The company maintains a drug and alcohol-free workplace. Employees must not report to work under the influence of substances that impair judgment. Testing may be conducted post-incident or with reasonable cause.

## SECTION 5 — TIME OFF & LEAVE MANAGEMENT

### 5.1 Annual Paid Leave

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per year (1.67 days/month). Leave requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance and are subject to manager approval.

### 5.2 Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per year. Sick leave beyond 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate. Unused sick leave does not carry over or get encashed.

### 5.3 Public Holidays

The company observes 12 mandatory public holidays per year. A regional holiday calendar is published by HR in the first week of January. Employees may swap public holidays for other culturally significant dates with manager approval.

### 5.4 Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to 5 days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling) and 2 days for extended family. Supporting documentation may be requested.

### 5.5 Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to 30 days per year may be requested for personal reasons. Approval requires HR and VP-level sign-off. Benefits continue during approved unpaid leave.

## SECTION 6 — PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

### 6.1 Goal Setting

Employees set OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) quarterly in alignment with team and company goals. OKRs are documented in the performance management system and reviewed at mid-quarter check-ins.

### 6.2 Continuous Feedback

Managers are expected to provide monthly 1:1 feedback sessions. Employees may also request peer feedback at any time through the performance portal. A culture of real-time, constructive feedback is strongly encouraged.

### 6.3 Annual Review Cycle

Formal performance reviews are conducted in December. Ratings are: 1-Unsatisfactory, 2-Needs Improvement, 3-Meets Expectations, 4-Exceeds Expectations, 5-Outstanding. Distribution guidelines are shared with managers each cycle.

### 6.4 Performance Improvement Plans

Employees rated 1 or 2 consecutively may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 60 to 90 days. PIPs include specific, measurable targets and weekly check-ins with HR and the manager.

---

## SECTION 7 — IT & DATA SECURITY

### 7.1 Acceptable Use Policy

Company systems and devices are for business use. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not compromise security, productivity, or the company's reputation.

### 7.2 Password & Authentication Standards

All accounts must use passwords of minimum 14 characters with MFA enabled. Passwords must be unique per service and stored only in the approved password manager (1Password Enterprise).

### 7.3 Data Handling

Employees handling Confidential or Restricted data must use encrypted channels (VPN + TLS). Sensitive data must never be stored on personal devices or sent via personal email accounts.

### 7.4 Incident Response Obligation

Every employee is obligated to report security anomalies, phishing attempts, or data loss events within 1 hour. Failure to report a known incident is treated as a policy violation.

---

## SECTION 8 — ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By continuing employment with the company, all employees acknowledge having read, understood, and agreed to abide by the policies outlined in this handbook. An electronic acknowledgment must be submitted via the HR portal upon joining and upon each annual update.
