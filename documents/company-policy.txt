POLICY 01 — INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Chunk ID: POLICY-2026-VDB-003::chunk::P01
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
This policy establishes the framework for protecting company information assets
from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Scope:
Applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, and third-party users who
access company systems, data, or infrastructure.

Requirements:
- All employees must complete annual cybersecurity awareness training.
- Access to systems follows the principle of least privilege (PoLP).
- Privileged access accounts must be reviewed and recertified every 90 days.
- All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled before deployment.
- USB storage devices are prohibited on company-managed machines.
- Remote access must be conducted exclusively over the corporate VPN.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all corporate accounts.

Violation Consequences:
First offense results in mandatory retraining. Repeat offenses or deliberate
violations may lead to termination and potential legal proceedings.

POLICY 02 — ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY (AUP)
Chunk ID: POLICY-2026-VDB-003::chunk::P02
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
To define appropriate use of company-provided technology, systems, and networks.

Permitted Uses:
- Business communications, documentation, and collaboration.
- Access to approved SaaS tools and internal applications.
- Limited personal use that does not interfere with work or consume excessive
  network bandwidth.

Prohibited Uses:
- Downloading or distributing pirated software, media, or copyrighted material.
- Accessing, storing, or transmitting offensive, discriminatory, or illegal content.
- Mining cryptocurrency on company systems or using company bandwidth.
- Using AI tools not approved by the IT Security team to process company data.
- Circumventing network controls, firewalls, or access restrictions.

Monitoring Notice:
Employees are informed that company systems are monitored for security and
compliance purposes. Activity logs may be reviewed by IT Security or Legal.

POLICY 03 — DATA PRIVACY POLICY
Chunk ID: POLICY-2026-VDB-003::chunk::P03
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
To ensure compliance with applicable data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, PDPA)
and safeguard personal data of employees, clients, and partners.

Data Subject Rights:
Individuals whose personal data is processed by the company have the right to:
- Access their personal data held by the company.
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Request deletion of data where no legal obligation to retain it exists.
- Object to automated decision-making or profiling.
- Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

Obligations for Employees Handling Personal Data:
- Collect only data that is necessary for the stated business purpose.
- Store personal data only in approved, access-controlled systems.
- Never transfer personal data to unauthorized third parties.
- Report any suspected personal data breach to the DPO within 1 hour.
- Complete GDPR and data handling training within 30 days of joining.

Data Protection Officer Contact:
dpo@corp.internal | ext. 3030

POLICY 04 — CODE OF BUSINESS ETHICS POLICY
Chunk ID: POLICY-2026-VDB-003::chunk::P04
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
To maintain the highest standards of ethical business conduct across all
operations, jurisdictions, and stakeholder interactions.

Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption:
Employees must not offer, receive, or facilitate bribes or kickbacks in any form
to influence business decisions. This applies to government officials, clients,
and vendors alike. Hospitality and gifts must not exceed $75 in value per
occasion and must be disclosed in the Gifts Register.

Fair Competition:
Employees must not engage in price-fixing, market allocation, bid-rigging, or
other anti-competitive practices. Any contact with competitor representatives
in potentially sensitive commercial contexts must be logged and reviewed by Legal.

Insider Trading:
Employees who have access to material non-public information (MNPI) about the
company or its publicly traded clients are strictly prohibited from trading in
related securities. Violations are a criminal offense.

Conflict of Interest Disclosure:
All actual, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest must be disclosed via
the annual COI Declaration Form submitted through the compliance portal. Ongoing
disclosure is required if circumstances change during the year.

POLICY 05 — REMOTE WORK POLICY
Chunk ID: POLICY-2026-VDB-003::chunk::P05
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
To establish standards for remote work that maintain productivity, security,
and collaboration across distributed teams.

Eligibility:
Remote work eligibility is defined in the employee's job description or
approved via a formal Remote Work Agreement signed by the employee, manager,
and HR. Not all roles qualify for remote work.

Security Requirements for Remote Employees:
- Must connect to internal systems only via the approved corporate VPN client.
- Home networks must use WPA3 or WPA2 encryption.
- Screens must not be visible to unauthorized individuals when working in
  public or shared spaces.
- Physical documents containing confidential data must be stored securely
  and shredded when no longer needed.

Equipment:
The company provides a standard remote work kit including a laptop, headset,
and webcam. Additional peripherals must be requested through IT. Employees are
responsible for the safe and proper use of all company-issued equipment.

Availability:
Remote employees must be reachable during core hours (10 AM to 4 PM, local
time) via Slack and must respond to messages within 2 hours during those hours.

POLICY 06 — DIVERSITY, EQUITY & INCLUSION POLICY
Chunk ID: POLICY-2026-VDB-003::chunk::P06
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
To foster a workplace where all individuals are respected, valued, and given
equal opportunity regardless of background or identity.

Commitments:
- The company sets annual diversity hiring targets reviewed by the Board.
- All interviewers must complete structured interview training before conducting
  hiring panels.
- Pay equity audits are conducted annually by an independent third party.
- An Employee Resource Group (ERG) program is funded with $10,000 per active
  ERG per year.
- Leadership development programs include representation benchmarks across
  gender, ethnicity, and disability dimensions.

Reporting Discrimination:
Employees who experience or witness discrimination should report it to
dei@corp.internal or through the anonymous ethics hotline. Retaliation against
anyone who files a good-faith complaint is strictly prohibited.

POLICY 07 — ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY POLICY
Chunk ID: POLICY-2026-VDB-003::chunk::P07
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
To minimize the company's environmental footprint and contribute to global
sustainability goals in alignment with the Paris Agreement.

Commitments:
- Achieve carbon neutrality in Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions by 2028.
- Reduce Scope 3 emissions by 30% from the 2023 baseline by 2030.
- All new office leases must meet LEED Silver or equivalent certification.
- Single-use plastics are banned in all company facilities globally.
- Employee business travel carbon footprint is offset via verified carbon
  credits for every trip exceeding 500 km.

Reporting:
Annual sustainability reports are published on the company intranet and external
website. Employees are encouraged to submit sustainability improvement ideas
via the Green Ideas portal at sustainability.corp.internal.

POLICY 08 — DISCIPLINARY POLICY
Chunk ID: POLICY-2026-VDB-003::chunk::P08
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
To provide a fair, consistent, and transparent process for addressing employee
misconduct or poor performance.

Levels of Disciplinary Action:
1. Verbal Warning — Documented in the HR system. First minor offense.
2. Written Warning — Formal notice with clear behavioral expectations.
3. Final Written Warning — Last step before termination for repeat offenses.
4. Suspension Without Pay — Applied during investigation of serious allegations.
5. Termination — For gross misconduct, policy violations, or repeated failures.

Gross Misconduct (Immediate Termination Without Notice):
- Theft, fraud, or financial dishonesty.
- Physical violence or threats in the workplace.
- Deliberate data breach or sabotage of company systems.
- Falsification of company records or credentials.
- Serious breach of confidentiality obligations.

Appeals Process:
Employees may appeal any disciplinary decision within 5 business days by
submitting a written appeal to hr@corp.internal. Appeals are reviewed by
an independent panel within 10 business days.

POLICY 09 — TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY
Chunk ID: POLICY-2026-VDB-003::chunk::P09
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
To ensure business travel is planned efficiently, cost-effectively, and in
compliance with financial controls.

Booking Requirements:
All travel must be booked via the corporate travel platform at least 7 days in
advance. Last-minute bookings require manager pre-approval via email.

Expense Limits (Per Day, USD):
- Meals: $75 domestic | $120 international
- Ground transport: Receipts required for amounts over $20
- Hotels: Up to $200/night domestic | $300/night international (pre-approved)

Prohibited Expenses:
- Minibar charges, in-room movies, or personal entertainment.
- Fines or penalties of any kind.
- Expenses for non-employees without specific VP approval.
- Class upgrades without Director-level written approval.

Submission Deadline:
All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the travel end date.
Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed without CFO approval.

POLICY 10 — AI TOOLS USAGE POLICY
Chunk ID: POLICY-2026-VDB-003::chunk::P10
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
To govern the responsible, secure, and ethical use of AI tools by employees
in the course of their work.

Approved AI Tools:
A list of approved AI tools is maintained by IT Security on the AI Tools
Registry at it.corp.internal/ai-tools. Employees must only use tools on this
list for work-related tasks involving company data.

Prohibited Actions:
- Uploading customer PII, financial data, or Restricted-classified company
  information into any AI tool (approved or otherwise) without explicit
  DPO sign-off.
- Using AI-generated content without human review before publication or delivery.
- Misrepresenting AI-generated work as entirely human-authored to clients.
- Training or fine-tuning external AI models using company proprietary data.

Accountability:
Employees remain fully accountable for any output generated by AI tools used
in their work. AI assistance does not transfer or reduce legal or professional
responsibility for accuracy, compliance, or quality.

Review Cadence:
This policy is reviewed every 6 months given the rapid pace of change in the
AI landscape. The latest version is always available at policy.corp.internal.
